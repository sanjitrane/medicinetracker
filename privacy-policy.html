Privacy Policy — Medicine Tracker

Last updated: September 24, 2026
Developer contact: sanjitrane12@gmail.com

Medicine Tracker ("the App") helps you keep track of medicines, dosage schedules, and refill/expiry reminders for yourself and people you care for (patients). This policy explains what data the App collects, how it is used, and your choices.

1. Data we collect

DataWhat it's forWhere it's stored
Phone number (yours)Signing in to the AppOn your device only (secure local storage)
Patient details you enter (name, an alert phone number)Organizing medicines per person and identifying who a dose reminder is forOn your device only (local database)
Medicine details you enter or scan (name, dosage, quantity, manufacturing/expiry dates, schedule)Tracking doses, refill and expiry remindersOn your device only (local database)
Photos of medicine packaging (only if you use the camera-scan feature)Automatically reading the medicine name and dates off the packaging, so you don't have to type themNot stored by the App. The photo is sent once to our OCR provider (see Section 2) to extract text, and is not saved afterward.
Dose confirmations and reminder times you setThe "today's doses" checklist and scheduling local alarm notificationsOn your device only (local database)

The App does not have a server or backend of its own. There is currently no user account system beyond the sign-in step above, and no analytics or advertising SDKs are included.

2. Sharing with third parties

The only data ever sent off your device is a photo you deliberately take of medicine packaging, and only when you use the "Scan Medicine" feature. That photo is sent to OpenAI (openai.com), which reads the text on the packaging and returns it to the App. OpenAI processes this request under its own privacy policy. The photo is not stored by the App before or after this request.

If you choose to enter medicines manually instead of scanning, no data ever leaves your device.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

3. Notifications

The App schedules local reminders (refill, expiry, and dose-time alarms) using your device's own notification system. These are generated and delivered entirely on-device — no reminder content is sent to us or to any third party.

4. Data about other people (patients)

If you add a patient who is not yourself (for example, a parent or child you care for), you are providing their name, an alert phone number, and their medicine information. This data is stored locally on your device in the same way as your own data. Please only add a patient's information if you are authorized to do so on their behalf.

5. Data retention and deletion

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you control it directly:

  • You can delete an individual medicine or patient from within the App.
  • Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data.
  • Scanned photos are never retained by the App in the first place — see Section 1.

6. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data directly from children. A parent or guardian may use the App to track a child's medicines as a "patient" record, as described in Section 4.

7. Security

Data is stored locally on your device using standard secure storage and a local database. Network requests to our OCR provider (Section 2) are made over HTTPS.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly. Continued use of the App after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to sanjitrane12@gmail.com.

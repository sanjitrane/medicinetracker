Medicine Tracker does not have a server or cloud account. Signing in with your phone number does not create an account on any Medicine Tracker server — it only unlocks the app on your device. Everything you enter (patients, medicines, doses, reminder times) is stored locally on that device, in the app's own storage.

How to delete your data

Uninstalling the app deletes everything. This is the complete and immediate way to remove all Medicine Tracker data from your device — your sign-in session, every patient record, every medicine, dose history, and reminder settings. There is nothing left on any server to separately delete, because nothing is ever uploaded there in the first place (see the exception in "What about scanned photos?" below).

  1. Long-press the Medicine Tracker app icon.
  2. Select Uninstall (or drag it to Uninstall).
  3. Confirm. All app data on this device is removed immediately.

You can also remove individual patients or medicines from within the app (Patients screen, or a medicine's own delete option) without uninstalling, if you only want to remove specific records rather than everything.

What about scanned photos?

If you used the camera-scan feature, the photo was sent once to OpenAI to read the text on the packaging, and was never stored by Medicine Tracker itself — see the Privacy Policy for details. There is no Medicine Tracker copy of that photo to delete. Any retention on OpenAI's side is governed by OpenAI's own privacy policy.

# Play Console → App content → category, target audience, content rating

Also filled out in the Play Console UI. Recommended answers below.

## App category
- **Category:** Medical (Play Store has a dedicated "Medical" category under Apps).
  If Medical isn't a good fit for your developer account type, "Health & Fitness"
  is the fallback.
- **Tags:** medicine reminder, medication tracker, pill reminder, dosage tracker.

## Target audience and content
- **Target age group:** 18 and over, or "Not for children" — do **not** opt into
  "Designed for Families." This app stores health-adjacent data about the account
  holder and about other people (patients), which the Families program and
  under-13 handling rules scrutinize heavily. Keep it general-audience.
- **Ads:** No ads in the app currently — answer "No."
- **In-app purchases:** None currently — answer "No."

## Content rating questionnaire (IARC)
Answer as a Reference/Productivity/Medical utility app with no user-generated
content shared with others, no violence, no sexual content, no gambling, no
user-to-user communication or chat. This combination should resolve to
**"Everyone" / PEGI 3** across all regional rating boards. Go through the actual
questionnaire in Play Console (it's rating-board-specific), but every "does your
app contain X" question should be answered No except the data-collection ones,
which should match `data-safety-answers.md`.

## Government apps / COVID-19 contact tracing
Answer **No** — this app is not affiliated with any government health authority.

## News apps declaration
Answer **No** — not a news app.
